Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to the details you share on byltrex.com. It is short because this website collects very little.

Who we are

This website is run by Byltrex. You can reach us about anything in this policy at muhammad@byltrex.com.

What we collect

  • What you type into the Get Started form: your name, company, email, and, if you choose to give them, your phone or WhatsApp number, the service you're interested in, your agent seats, your timeline and your message.
  • Basic technical data: like every website, the service that hosts this site (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts on this website. The fonts are hosted on our own site, so loading a page does not send your details to a font provider.

How we use it

We use what you send in the form only to reply to your request, discuss your setup and send you a quote. We do not sell your details, rent them out, or add you to a marketing list.

Who receives it

  • Web3Forms, the service that delivers the form to our inbox.
  • Our email provider, where your message is stored once it arrives.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep it

We keep your form message for as long as we need it to respond to you and follow up on your request. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

Email muhammad@byltrex.com to see, correct or delete the details you sent us. We'll reply within one business day.

Do Not Track

Because this website does not track visitors across sites or over time, browser "Do Not Track" signals do not change how it works.

Client call data

This policy covers this website only. How we handle call recordings and lead data for clients who use our bots or dialers is set out in each client agreement.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
