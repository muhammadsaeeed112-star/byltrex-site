How AI fronter bots work inside VICIdial

Short answer

An AI fronter bot logs into your VICIdial as a remote agent. Your campaign sends it answered calls, it asks your qualifying questions, then it either transfers the customer to your verifier in-group or writes a status such as A, DNC or DNQ back to the lead. Every call is recorded with its outcome.

An AI fronter does the same job as a human fronter: open the call, ask a couple of qualifying questions, and only pass real, qualified customers to your verifiers. Here is what actually happens inside your dialer when a bot does it.

1. The bot is just another agent in your campaign

VICIdial has a feature called Remote Agents. A remote agent is a user that never opens the agent screen. Instead, VICIdial sends its calls to a phone number or extension. That is exactly how a fronter bot plugs in.

Each bot gets its own remote-agent user and an extension. That extension is routed over a SIP carrier to the bot's server. To your campaign, the bot looks like any other agent sitting READY. Your lists, hopper, dial method and calling hours stay exactly as they are.

2. Machines get filtered twice

Before a call ever reaches the bot, VICIdial can run answering machine detection (AMD) on it. AMD catches a lot of voicemail greetings, but no AMD is perfect. Some machines slip through, and a badly tuned AMD can hang up on real people who answer slowly.

That is why a good fronter bot does its own second check. It listens to how the call opens: long greetings, "leave a message after the tone", call-screening apps asking who is calling, and IVR menus. Those calls end on the bot as answering machines and never reach your verifiers. In your reports, VICIdial marks machines its AMD caught as AA, and the bot marks the ones it caught as A, so you can see which filter did the work.

3. The conversation stays short on purpose

A fronter's job is not to sell. It is to find out, fast, whether this is a real person who qualifies. So the bot plays a short intro, asks one or two qualifying questions, and listens. Speech-to-text turns each answer into text, and the bot's rules decide what happens next.

The best scripts are boring: a clear intro, the disclosures your compliance team requires, and the minimum questions needed to know whether the customer belongs with a verifier. Everything else is the verifier's job.

4. Every call ends with an outcome in your dialer

When the bot is done, one of two things happens:

  • The customer qualifies XFER and the call is transferred into your verifier in-group. Done right, the verifier's screen shows the real lead record, not a blank one.
  • The customer does not, and the bot writes a status back to the lead through VICIdial's API, the same way a human fronter dispositions a call: A for machines, DNC for "don't call me", a custom DNQ status (or NI) for non-qualifiers, and so on.

That write-back matters more than people think. Without it, the lead keeps its old status, your recycling rules call it again, and your reports show nothing about what the bot actually did.

5. Recordings are how you trust it

You should never have to take a bot's word for anything. Every call it takes should be recorded and tagged with the outcome, so you can pull up any transfer or any DNQ and hear exactly what the customer said. That is also how the script gets better: you listen to the calls that went wrong and fix the question that caused them.

A note on compliance. The FCC treats AI-generated voices as "artificial voice" under the TCPA. Consent for the numbers you dial, disclosures and calling-hour rules still apply, and they stay with the call center. A fronter bot is a tool on your floor, not a way around those rules. What to ask before you buy a fronter bot

  • Does it run inside my VICIdial, or does it need a separate system and a second set of lists?
  • What happens when it doesn't understand the customer? Ask exactly what the bot does next: re-ask, end the call or pass it on, and listen to recordings of those calls.
  • Does every outcome get written back to the lead, so my recycling and reports still work?
  • Can I hear every call? Recordings, tagged by outcome, on demand.
  • Will we run live test calls together before any real list goes out?

If a vendor can answer all five clearly, you are talking to people who understand a calling floor.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI fronter bot?

An AI fronter bot is software that does a call center fronter's job: it opens the call, asks a few qualifying questions and transfers only live, qualified customers to a verifier. In VICIdial it logs in as a remote agent, so it works inside the campaigns you already run.

How does an AI fronter bot connect to VICIdial?

Each bot is a VICIdial remote agent with its own user and extension. The extension is routed over a SIP carrier to the bot's server, so your campaign sends answered calls to the bot like any other agent and gets the result written back to the lead.

What happens to calls the bot doesn't transfer?

The bot writes a status back to the lead through VICIdial's API, for example A for answering machines, DNC for do-not-call requests or a custom DNQ status for non-qualifiers, so your recycling rules and reports stay accurate.
