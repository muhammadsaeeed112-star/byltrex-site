FAQ

Questions call‑center owners ask first.

What is an AI fronter bot?

An AI fronter bot is software that does a fronter's job on an outbound call: it opens the call, asks a few qualifying questions and transfers only live, qualified customers to your verifiers. Ours log into your VICIdial as remote agents, so they work inside the campaigns you already run.

Is an AI fronter TCPA-safe?

Byltrex provides the technology. Consent for your lists and compliance with TCPA and state rules stay your responsibility. The FCC treats AI-generated voices as "artificial voice" under the TCPA. We build the script to the rules and disclosures you give us, and every call is recorded. This is not legal advice; check with your compliance counsel.

Which dialers do you work with?

Our fronter bots are built for VICIdial and log in as remote agents, so your campaigns, lists and in-groups stay as they are. Running something else? Tell us in the form and we'll let you know honestly whether it fits.

How long does setup take?

It depends on your dialer, your script and how many bots you need. You'll get a clear timeline with your quote, and we run live test calls with your team before going live.

Who owns the leads and recordings?

You do. Your leads stay in your dialer, and the recordings and call outcomes belong to you.

What hours do the bots call?

Whatever your VICIdial campaign is set to. Calling hours stay under your control in your own dialer.
